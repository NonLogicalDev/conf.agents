# Evidence, Blockers, and Completion

Read this reference when updating progress, recording a blocker, or deciding
whether a plan is complete.

## Record actual progress

Append a dated entry to the numbered plan's `Work Log` for each meaningful
investigation, implementation milestone, artifact change, decision, check,
blocker transition, or material user correction. State what changed, what
is complete, its actual evidence, what remains, and the next action.

Group related commands into the milestone they support. Keep independent
events distinguishable. Preserve the existing chronological history; do
not replace meaningful work with a single checked task or expand routine
commands into a transcript.

Distinguish a proposed action from work that actually happened. Do not infer
an overall passing result from a partial check.

## Keep artifacts and decisions current

Maintain the numbered plan's `Artifacts` when its files, tests, branches,
commits, pull requests, or other outputs change. Record each verified type,
state, contents, location, and relevant exact revision. Mark an unknown or
planned detail directly.

Record a material implementation choice in `Decisions` with its rationale.
Preserve a superseded choice and explain what changed. Update the plan's
`Validation` with the actual check, expected result, observed outcome, and
what that outcome establishes.

Use the epic's `EXEC_STATE.md` for the overall epic and its produced
external artifacts. Do not use it to replace the numbered plan's work log,
artifact inventory, decisions, or validation.

## Record blockers precisely

For a real blocker, record:

- the specific planned action that is blocked;
- the observed condition preventing it;
- the actual resolution or focused next investigation; and
- the result that will show that the action can continue.

When the answer is unknown, say so. Do not invent permissions, approval
routes, owners, results, or external requests.

Keep unrelated accepted work visible. Remove a blocker only when evidence
shows that its actual condition has cleared.

## Keep learnings useful

Record decisions, assumptions that changed, relevant discoveries, and
unexpected outcomes when they affect the plan or future execution. Keep
them concise and connect each learning to the task or decision it informs.

## Check completion

A plan is complete when the accepted work satisfies its stated
completion criteria and the required evidence actually exists.

Record remaining gaps honestly. A completed step, reviewer opinion, or
passing partial check does not establish that the whole plan is complete.
